# Personal Project - Nick Schumacher
## Overview
In this game you controll a Fish and have to survive as long as possible against other fish, who are constantly trying to attack you.
You have a HP and an energy bar. Your HP restores over time and your energy drains overtime. If you are out of energy, you get slowed down and wont be able to boost.
To restore energy, you have to eat other fish. You grow with each kill and get faster aswell.

## Features
- Different type of fish, with different stats: size, speed, hp, dmg
- Enemy Fish are either fleeing or hunting, based on their and the other fish size. Also damage is scaled based on that
- Low Poly artstyle with a fixed camera view

## Controls
- Movement WASD
- Boost Shift
- Bite Space

## Installation
1. Clone/download the repository.
2. Open in Unity (6000.0.51f1).
3. Press Play to run.
